THE CONTRAST THAT MATTERS
  carrier rig, 4 cores at 300 MHz (the lowest OPP), pinned:
    UVLO reset after 743 s; VBAT 4.2-4.4 V steady with a transient sample at
    3.421 V - right at the pm8941 lockout threshold
  real phone, 4 cores at ~1.9 GHz average:
    60 s clean at 82 C, VBAT steady just under 4.0 V

  Same SoC, same speed bin, roughly six times the aggregate clock on the phone.
  The rig fails at the lowest operating point the silicon has, while the phone
  is fine at the highest. Every reset on the rig during the ceiling search
  reported poff=0x2000 (UVLO) - at 1497.6, 883.2, 422.4 and 300 MHz alike.

  A battery is a low-impedance buffer; the carrier feeds VBAT directly. Since
  the rig's steady-state voltage is healthy and only transients reach lockout,
  the signature points at feed impedance (wiring, connector, insufficient bulk
  capacitance) rather than an average-power limit.

  Caveat, stated deliberately: the phone runs the vendor 3.4 kernel, so this
  compares platform-and-supply, not kernel-versus-kernel. Flashing 6.16 and
  then 6.18 onto this phone is what isolates the kernel, and it is the next
  measurement.
